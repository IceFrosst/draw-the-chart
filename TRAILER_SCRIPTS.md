## Rules that apply to every cut

1. **Every number is real** and reproducible from seed `445625`. Nothing is invented.
2. **The end card carries** `ILLUSTRATIVE · SANDBOX · MOST ROUNDS LOSE`. Not a legal
   afterthought — a trailer that only shows winning is misleading and, for a stake-based
   product, a problem with advertising regulators in most jurisdictions.
3. **No language implying expected returns.** "Paid on how close you got" is fine.
   "Make money" and "earn" are not.
4. **Every numeral is set in JetBrains Mono.** This is most of what separates the look from
   a gambling ad.
5. **Two shots carry the film**: the draw and the reveal. If a cut runs long, take the time
   from everywhere else first.
